# FILTIX Charts v0.4.0 — synchronized analysis and replay

Status: accepted for the private local v0.4.0 release. Functional, performance and independent evidence reviews passed. Private local milestone, 2026-09-09.

The seven-package cohort adds optional @filtix/analysis. Charts can share loaded time ranges and cursors across different calendars of available bars. Comparison series use an exact common price baseline. A deterministic controller reveals complete historical candles, with play, pause, step, seek, speed and disposal.

The original /analysis.html workspace combines linked BTC/ETH price charts, volume, EMA20 and indexed BTC/ETH/SOL comparison. Its fixed synthetic hourly sample intentionally omits some asset timestamps. Seeking rebuilds every derived series from the revealed prefix; future bars do not reach charts or calculators. Explicit browser-local Save/Load validates the entire versioned workspace before applying it and always loads paused.

## Integration and limits

See the [analysis guide](../ANALYSIS.md), [analysis contract](../ANALYSIS-CONTRACT.md), [chart synchronization contract](../CHART-SYNC-CONTRACT.md), [design](../../SOURCE-DISTRIBUTION.md#omitted-development-materials) and [implementation plan](../../SOURCE-DISTRIBUTION.md#omitted-development-materials).

Synchronization uses loaded semantic endpoints. Missing timestamps remain missing; exact cursors hide where no point exists, and optional nearest matching selects a real point. One-point ranges may expand to a neighbor. One active group per chart is supported. A destroyed group releases its own subscriptions and leaves the last viewport/cursor. Ordinary chart time domains are immutable.

Comparison requires positive present prices and an exact common baseline; it does not adjust prices, interpolate or carry values forward. Replay reveals whole candles without inventing an intrabar path, catch-up clock or simulated trades. This workspace uses a fixed labelled synthetic dataset; the separate live market uses the public datafeed adapter.

The original v0.4 archives, commits and release tag remain in the separate development archive.
