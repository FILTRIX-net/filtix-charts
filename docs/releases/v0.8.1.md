## Behavior

Dense series titles now stay inside their own pane and left of the price axis. The existing scene Canvas measures titles in the theme font, wraps whole titles, shortens a single long Unicode title safely, and displays an exact `+N` count for omitted titles. It clips final pixels to the pane/plot rectangle, retains insertion order and series colors, and keeps committed titles in PNG export. No extra DOM legend, canvas, handler, timer, full-history read or series recreation was introduced. The independent cursor overlay still moves without a scene redraw. The Indicators editor retains the complete study names and settings.

`ChartOptions.legend` accepts `visible?: boolean` and `maxRows?: number` (integer 1–4), defaulting to visible and two rows. Partial option patches merge copied settings. Invalid nested values or keys reject atomically. Zero-sized or maximized-away panes suppress titles and recover after resize. This patch does not add dynamic cursor values to the legend or redesign axis-label collisions. See the [API guide](../API.md) and [quality specification](../../SOURCE-DISTRIBUTION.md#omitted-development-materials).
